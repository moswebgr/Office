Welcome to MailNest

Connect Outlook to view your mailbox. You can also compose a local draft without connecting.

Privacy: MailNest requests Microsoft Graph delegated permissions to read mail, manage drafts, and send messages. Mail is loaded from Microsoft when you connect; local drafts are saved in this browser. This static site does not run its own mail server. Disconnect/sign out through your Microsoft account and revoke app access in your Microsoft account if needed.
